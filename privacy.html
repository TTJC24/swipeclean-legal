Privacy Policy — SwipeClean

Effective date: November 8, 2026

SwipeClean is a camera-roll cleaner. This policy explains what data the app handles — in short: your photos stay on your device, and we collect nothing.

Data we collect

None. SwipeClean does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, and no tracking. The publisher operates no server that your data could be sent to.

Photo Library access

SwipeClean asks for access to your Photo Library for one purpose: to show you your own photos, on your device, so you can review them and choose what to keep and what to delete.

  • Your photos are displayed and processed (including duplicate and blurry detection) entirely on your device.
  • Your photos are never uploaded, copied, or sent anywhere by SwipeClean.
  • If you use iCloud Photos, your library syncs through your own Apple account under Apple's terms, as it does without SwipeClean. SwipeClean is not involved in that sync and receives none of it.
  • If you grant limited ("selected photos") access, SwipeClean works only with the photos you selected. You can change or revoke Photo Library access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

Deleting photos

Nothing is deleted by a swipe alone. Photos you mark are shown in a review grid, and deletion happens only after you confirm, using the standard iOS Photos confirmation. Deleted photos are moved by iOS to the Recently Deleted album in your Photos app, where they remain recoverable for 30 days before iOS deletes them permanently. You can recover them — or delete them permanently sooner — at any time in the Photos app. SwipeClean does not keep a copy of anything you delete.

Data on your device

The app stores a small amount of working data only on your device — swipe decisions, session progress, cleanup totals and streaks, and settings — using Apple's on-device storage. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

SwipeClean Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through In-App Purchase. We never see or handle your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs the purchase itself.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Children

SwipeClean is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children — because we do not collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
