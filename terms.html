The service

SwipeClean helps you review photos in your own Photo Library, decide what to keep and what to delete, and see how much storage space you have freed. It runs entirely on your device: there is no account, and no SwipeClean server receives your photos or data.

Deleting photos — your responsibility

You are in control of every deletion, and you are responsible for your choices:

  • No photo is deleted by a swipe alone. Marked photos are shown to you in a review grid, and deletion happens only after you confirm the selection and approve the standard iOS Photos confirmation.
  • Deleting with SwipeClean uses the iOS Photos system: deleted photos move to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app and remain recoverable there for 30 days. After that period iOS deletes them permanently. You can also empty Recently Deleted yourself at any time, which deletes them permanently right away.
  • Review your selections carefully before confirming. The publisher is not responsible for photos you confirm for deletion that are later permanently deleted by iOS after the Recently Deleted period, or that you permanently delete yourself from Recently Deleted.
  • For photos you cannot afford to lose, keep a separate backup (for example, iCloud Photos or another backup) before cleaning.
